# Music

The music is the structure. Picture energy follows musical energy. A cut on every beat, for the whole piece, becomes a metronome and stops meaning anything.

Read the sections first: open, groove, lift, drop or chorus, bridge, end. Assign the visual change to the musical change. Save the strongest picture for the strongest change.

Pick one sync and keep it:

- Hard sync, for a drop or a hit: the cut or the visual impact lands on the transient, or a frame before it. Review by eye and ear.
- Phrase sync, for an explainer or a calmer piece: the story beat lands on the phrase, not on every kick.
- Against the beat, only for tension or irony.
- Silence or a ducked bed when a line, a reveal, or a joke has to land. Speech stays intelligible.

Let a phrase finish. Anticipation starts before the hit. The payoff lands on the hit or just before it. Do not cut the unstable first or last frames of a shot just to chase a transient.

Repetition needs a change: subject, direction, size, or meaning. The same frame on the same beat is not a motif.

On the plan: `music_id` is a file they imported, `music_volume` under 1 when people are talking. Omit `transition` except where a section changes. The one push, if any, is `end_scale` on the lift or the drop, and only on a hold whose motion is `none`. Leave it off every other slot. Captions do not pop on every beat.
