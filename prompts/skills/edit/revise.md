# Revise

Translate a vague note into one timeline change. Then make that change. Do not describe it.

- "Punchier" can mean: drop the setup, shorten a shot, bring the proof earlier, cut a pause, or shorten a caption. It does not mean a zoom on every hold.
- "More cinematic" can mean: fewer graphics, a longer hold on the suspense, a wider open, a stronger contrast between sections, or a rise toward one button. It is not one grade and it is not one push.
- "More premium" can mean: slower pace, fewer transitions, a restrained move, and the product held long enough to read.
- "More social" can mean: the hook is a person or an action, the setup is shorter, the claim is direct, and the captions are readable with the sound off.

Name the action: keep, cut, replace, move, trim, extend, cover, or duck. Include the reason and what the viewer should get.

A `fix:` line is a problem. Correct it with one `revise_edit`. A `note:` line is taste. It does not need a tool unless they asked for that change.

On the plan: `revise_edit` patches the slot. `slot` is the 0-based index. Change the field that answers the note: `source_in`, `duration`, `transition`, `end_scale`, `speed`, `grade`, `cover`, `fade_in`, or `fade_out`. Leave every other field as it was. A revision rebuilds the timeline, so a graphic waits until the last revision.
