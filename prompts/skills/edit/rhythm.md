# Rhythm

Pace is how fast the meaning changes, not how short the shots are. A long hold is fast when the suspense is changing. A rapid run is slow when every frame says the same thing.

Cut faster when the viewer already understands the subject and the beat is energy, the footage repeats an action, or the music is the structure. Hold longer when the viewer must read a screen, a caption, or a face, the voiceover is dense, or the tone is calm, technical, or documentary.

A straight cut is the default. A dissolve is time passing, a memory, or a comparison, and only when that is the reason. One wipe (`wipe_left`) is one join, and only after the piece has a visual language that wants it. Omit `transition` for a cut. The names that exist are `dissolve`, `fade_black`, `fade_white`, `wipe_left`, and `slide_up`. Any other name is the wrong join. Never solve an unclear story by adding a transition. One treatment on a join. A dissolve, a push, and a speed change are three ideas.

J-cut: the next sound starts before its picture, when the next thought should pull. L-cut: the words hold while the picture moves to the proof, the reaction, or the insert. A sound bridge connects images that do not match. An audio hole is more distracting than a hard picture cut.

A push is a choice, not a default. `end_scale` above 1 moves in. `end_scale` below 1 eases out. Set it only when this shot's job is to reveal or to intensify, and the shot list motion is `none`. `toward` is already a push. `away` is already a pull. `l2r` and `r2l` are already traveling. Leave `end_scale` off those. Most slots omit it. `ease` `out` when the move should arrive, `in` when it should leave, and omit `ease` to ease both ways. `speed` is a hit on an action, not a style.

If the piece is music-led, pick one sync: on the hits, on the phrase, against the beat, or silence under the line. Do not cut every beat. Let the anticipation start before the hit.

Review once in your head: does the picture make sense with the sound down, do the words make sense with the picture covered, and can a caption be read at this pace. Captions follow the phrase. Do not animate every word.

On the plan: put `end_scale` on the shots this page names and omit it on the rest. Put `transition` on the slot after the join that needs it. `fade_in` and `fade_out` only on the open and the close, unless this piece's shape said otherwise.
