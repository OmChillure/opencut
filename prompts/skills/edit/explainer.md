# Explainer

The viewer should understand one idea. Each beat proves or clarifies the line. A shot that only looks good does not earn its time.

Shape:

- Open on the surprising problem, the before, or the after. The first seconds say why this matters.
- Give only the context the next beat needs.
- One idea per beat. The picture proves the words. It does not repeat them as a synonym.
- Show how the parts connect.
- Close on the sentence, the implication, or the action. Do not add a new idea there.

A hold stays while the viewer is still reading the idea. Cut at the earliest frame where the idea has landed and the next beat can carry it. Drop ums, retakes, and a sentence that says the same thing again.

A cutaway covers a jump or shows the thing being named. It does not interrupt a thought that is still unfinished.

On the plan: excerpts in this order. `source_in` is the frame the beat starts. `duration` ends when the beat has done its job. Omit `transition` for a hard cut. `dissolve` only when time or place changes.
