# Trailer

The viewer should feel the world, then the disruption, then want the rest. Escalation is the structure. A trailer is not a summary of every moment.

Shape:

- The world or the premise, in one image.
- The disruption.
- Images that get more consequential. Do not spend the strongest image first.
- One or two signature moments.
- A button: the joke, the shock, the name, or the question left open.

Reserve the strongest visual change for the strongest turn. Repetition is a motif only when the meaning changes the second time. The same shot again, with the same meaning, is padding.

A straight cut is the default. One `wipe_left` or `slide_up` is one moment, and only when the piece has already taught that language. Do not use one to hide a weak order. Any other transition name is the wrong join.

On the plan: fewer excerpts than a documentary, chosen for consequence rather than coverage. The one push, if any, is `end_scale` on the disruption or the button, and only on a hold whose motion is `none`. Leave grade empty unless a frame you saw is a different light from the hero of that scene.
