# Ad

One claim. The viewer should know it is for them, see the proof, and get one next step. A pile of features is not an ad.

Shape:

- Hook: a problem, a contradiction, a person to camera, the product in action, or a before and after. The first shot is already the idea. A static title card is not the hook.
- A signal that this is for the viewer.
- One claim.
- Proof or a demo.
- The offer or the next step, after the proof.

Faster is useful when the viewer already has the idea and the next shot adds a new one. A run of shots that say the same thing is slow, however short they are.

Show the product or the use earlier than you would in a trailer. The first frame has to communicate without sound if the captions are on.

On the plan: excerpts in this order, inside the duration they asked for. Hard cuts. `dissolve` only for a before and after that is a change of time. `end_scale` belongs on the one shot whose job is the reveal, and only when that shot's motion is `none`. Leave it off the rest. Keep the caption line readable. Do not animate every word.
