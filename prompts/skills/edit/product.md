# Product

The viewer should see the thing in use and believe the claim. Beauty without use does not earn its time.

Shape:

- Pain, or the outcome they want.
- The product in a hand, on a screen, or in the place it is used. Not a beauty shot by itself.
- One feature per beat, tied to what the person gets.
- Proof: the result, the comparison, the screen, or the demonstration.
- The next step, once. Do not stack a second pitch after it.

Reject a shot that raises a question the rest of the piece cannot answer. Keep a handsome shot only when it carries the use or the proof.

Match the product across cuts: color, shape, logo, and what the screen shows. If a cut makes the product look like a different object, cover it, trim it, or use another imported angle.

On the plan: excerpts in this order. `source_in` is the action, not the head of the file. `duration` ends when the proof is visible. Omit `transition` unless the place changes. Leave a slot `grade` empty unless you saw the frame and the product color is wrong.
