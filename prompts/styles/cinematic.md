# Cinematic

Average shot: hook 2–3s, body 4–6s, end 3–4s. Frame stays the project's, unless they asked for vertical.

Cut on action and on the blink. Wide, then close. A match cut keeps the motion direction. A new angle of the same moment is a hard cut. A scene change may dissolve for under a second. J and L cuts are allowed: let the next line start under the previous picture, or hold the picture into the next line.

Leave a clear frame at grade zero. The model that looked at the frame sets a correction only when the frame is dark, flat, or off-color, and shots in the same light share it. Call `see` and set that slot's `grade` only when you want a different correction. Do not use mono unless they asked for black and white. A slow push (ease in_out, end_scale about 1.08) on a hold. Speed ramps only on an action beat.

End each talking slot on the last word. Leave a short tail after that word. Do not hold into the next sentence or into dead air.

Fades are not only the open and the close when this guide is attached.
