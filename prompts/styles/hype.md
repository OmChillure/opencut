# Hype

Average shot: hook under 1.5s, body 1–2s, end under 2s. Vertical when they said reel or tiktok.

Hard cuts on the beat. Whip-pans and speed ramps on the hits. Jump a shot size (wide to close) rather than matching it. Dissolves are rare. Smash the open.

Grade: high contrast, a little saturation. Grain is fine. No slow fades in the body.
